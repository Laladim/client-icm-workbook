# What belongs in each folder

| Folder | Information and evidence | Common questions | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| company | engagement, business SOPs, company onboarding, staff/delegation, existing clients, official accounts, service agreement and handover | What are we hired to do? Who does what? Which file is current? | reference existing company/client records; restrict personal/commercial data |
| brand | purpose, vision, mission, values; target audience, competitors and difference; character/voice, message and visuals; before/during/after marketing | Who are we for? What may we promise? How should this sound? | preserve undecided strategy and actual approvals; aspirations are not product proof |
| customer-evidence | demo/discovery calls, customer profiles, objections, case studies, testimonials, one-line praise and consent | What do real buyers ask? Which proof may be named or quoted? | a prospect is not a customer outcome; quotes, permissions and source identity remain exact |
| offers | product/service catalog, current capabilities and limits, FAQs, launch/change signals and online coverage | What changed, is it released, and where must it be communicated? | product/service owner verifies facts; a plan or announcement is not current behavior |
| channels | platform strategy, blog, newsletter/email, video, LinkedIn/social, website/CRO, SEO/AI visibility, paid media and events | Which channels serve this audience and business goal? Who owns production? | select channels by scope; bind existing owners; no inherited live-action permission |
| performance | KPI definitions, baselines, periods, sales/conversion events, CRM and native channel evidence, attribution and reporting | What happened commercially? What can these numbers prove? | traffic is not revenue; aggregates cannot establish individual customer outcomes |
| monitoring | source/owner map, cadence, evidence age, gaps, action permissions, corrections and maintenance | What is missing or stale? Who updates it? What action is blocked? | working links, scheduled jobs, successful refreshes and actual publication are different states |

The client workspace is an umbrella. Only its onboarding follows a required sequence. A content-producing workflow keeps its own stages and owner. Read the local CONTEXT.md and one relevant page; do not load the entire client for each task. One answer belongs in one canonical file, with links where another job uses it.
