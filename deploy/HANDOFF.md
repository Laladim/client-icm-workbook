# Client handoff and optional live pilot

Before handoff, show the exact approved brief and accepted workspace digests; owners and delegates; current source inventory and gaps; selected channels and their role; privacy/retention rules; measurement definitions; and the maintenance/contact rhythm. The client should know where to enter without the original chat.

Use one small reversible task for a pilot. Name the task, system, read/write range, approved human, tool access, expected artifact, meaningful success/refusal cases, readback evidence, and recovery. Record measured results and unresolved gaps. Do not infer general reliability from one successful pilot.

A connector or account invitation is optional. This template does not install integrations or background jobs. A planned cadence is not a successful refresh. A draft, queue row, delivered PDF or script is not online publication. Keep every send, publish, spend or production-change decision with the configured human/owner.

At review, compare source completeness, research/drafting time, rework, approval delay, factual corrections, publication gaps and the agreed commercial measure against the starting baseline. Define who checks sources and how a failed check is retried. For offboarding, hand over accepted files and ownership, revoke access through each account owner, and follow the client's retention/deletion decisions. Never silently delete or transfer accounts.
