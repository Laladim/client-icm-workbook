## Engagement, outcomes and scope (all engagements)
- **company.description** (Core). What does this client do, for whom, and in which markets? Establish the business before discussing formats. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.contact** (Core). Who is the day-to-day client contact and who can make decisions? Avoid unclear delegation and repeated approval requests. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.services** (Core). Which digital-marketing services are in scope, and which are excluded? Define the actual job and avoid unpriced work. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.success** (Core). What business outcome matters most, what is its baseline, and what would success look like? Connect content to a meaningful business result. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.deadlines** (Core). What launch dates, seasonal events, milestones and dependencies matter? Make delivery and campaign planning realistic. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.budget** (Optional). What budget or time capacity is available for content, tools, production and promotion? Keep the plan feasible; record confidential figures privately. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.deliverables** (Core). What deliverables, turnaround, revision limits and acceptance criteria were agreed? Make the service agreement operational. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.approver** (Core). Who approves the onboarding brief and workspace? Name a real human for decisions the agent cannot make. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.timezone** (Core). What timezone, working days, meeting rhythm and response times should we use? Prevent scheduling and expectation errors. File: `company/engagement.md`.
- **engagement.escalation** (Optional). Who handles urgent incidents, corrections, complaints and approval delays? Give uncertain or urgent work a safe next step. File: `company/engagement.md`.

## Company knowledge and operating responsibilities (all engagements)
- **company.onboarding** (Core). Which company overview, onboarding deck, service agreement and business SOPs already exist? Reference current records before creating replacements. File: `company/operations.md`.
- **company.people** (Core). Who does what across leadership, sales, product, support, marketing, design and technical work? Build a delegation and handoff map. File: `company/operations.md`.
- **company.customers** (Core). Where is the existing client/customer list and what profile fields can marketing access? Learn segments while respecting privacy and permissions. File: `company/operations.md`.
- **company.accounts** (Core). Which website, social accounts and listings are official, and who owns each? Prevent work on an abandoned or unofficial account. File: `company/operations.md`.
- **company.workflows** (Core). Which existing workflows or agencies already own this work? List their entry files or procedures if known. Bind the current owner rather than creating a competing process. File: `company/operations.md`.
- **company.handover** (Optional). What knowledge is undocumented, who can cover each role, and where do accepted changes get recorded? Make the workspace useful for another team member. File: `company/operations.md`.

## Brand strategy and target audience (all engagements)
- **brand.purpose** (Core). Why does this business exist, beyond its products or services? Anchor the internal brand. File: `brand/strategy/internal-brand.md`.
- **brand.vision** (Optional). What future is the business working toward, and which parts are aspirations? Keep future ambition separate from current capabilities. File: `brand/strategy/internal-brand.md`.
- **brand.mission** (Optional). What does the business commit to doing now? Describe the present mission. File: `brand/strategy/internal-brand.md`.
- **brand.values** (Optional). Which values should shape behavior, and what evidence shows them? Keep values concrete without inventing outcomes. File: `brand/strategy/internal-brand.md`.
- **audience.primary** (Core). Who is the primary audience, buyer, user and decision maker? Choose whom the work serves; do not guess from an example. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.
- **audience.jobs** (Core). What situation makes them seek help, what job are they trying to finish, and what blocks it? Find recognizable buying triggers and useful content. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.
- **audience.research** (Core). What do we know from customer research, and which fears, beliefs or segments are still hypotheses? Separate researched evidence from assumptions. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.
- **audience.exclusions** (Optional). Who is a poor fit, and which markets, claims or use cases are out of scope? Avoid attracting the wrong demand. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.
- **brand.competitors** (Optional). Which alternatives do customers consider, including doing nothing or doing it in-house? Frame competitive research without assuming weaknesses. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.
- **brand.difference** (Core). What difference can the client prove, and what should the audience remember? Ground positioning in evidence. File: `brand/strategy/positioning.md`.

## Voice, message and visual identity (all engagements)
- **brand.character** (Optional). What personality or archetype fits the brand, and how should it behave? Translate character into writing choices without forcing a framework. File: `brand/identity/character.md`.
- **brand.voice** (Core). How should the brand sound? Give accepted and rejected examples, terminology and style rules. Make voice reproducible. File: `brand/identity/character.md`.
- **brand.messages** (Core). Which offer wording, core messages, promise, stories, name and tagline are already approved? Preserve actual approvals and leave undecided blocks open. File: `brand/identity/message.md`.
- **brand.claims** (Core). Which phrases, comparisons, guarantees or claims need evidence or special review? Avoid making unapproved promises. File: `brand/identity/message.md`.
- **brand.visuals** (Core). Where are current logos, colors, fonts, photography rules, templates and asset rights? Use the right design files without inventing a visual identity. File: `brand/identity/visual.md`.
- **brand.languages** (Optional). Which languages, reading level, accessibility and localization requirements apply? Make the content usable by the intended audience. File: `brand/identity/character.md`.

## Before, during and after the sale (all engagements)
- **journey.before** (Core). How do new prospects find the business, become engaged, and recognize a need? Describe discovery and acquisition without confusing traffic with demand. File: `brand/marketing/before.md`.
- **journey.capture** (Core). What action captures a lead or starts a purchase, and what value is offered? Connect discovery to the next useful step. File: `brand/marketing/during.md`.

- **journey.conversion** (Core). What is the conversion path, who owns it, and what defines a qualified lead or sale? Define the actual commercial event. File: `brand/marketing/during.md`.
- **journey.after** (Core). How are customers onboarded, supported, retained, expanded or invited to refer? Include customer experience, lifetime value and referrals. File: `brand/marketing/after.md`.
- **journey.content** (Optional). What content is needed at each step, what CTA fits, and what should never be pushed prematurely? Map content to the journey rather than copying one format everywhere. File: `brand/marketing/during.md`.

## Customer evidence and proof permissions (all engagements)
- **evidence.calls** (Core). Where are demo, discovery or sales calls, interviews, support questions and loss reasons? Find the actual language and questions behind stronger content. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.profiles** (Optional). Which customer profiles, use cases and buying-group details are current? Avoid mixing different audiences or prospect and customer states. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.case-studies** (Core). Which case studies exist, what was the result, and where are the source and client approval? Keep exact evidence and publication consent connected. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.testimonials** (Core). Which testimonials and one-line praises may be quoted, named or paraphrased, and under what permission? A proof format is not permission to invent or shorten a quote. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.results** (Optional). Which metrics, before/after claims or screenshots have reproducible evidence and an approved time window? Do not combine unrelated results or turn estimates into outcomes. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.gaps** (Optional). What proof is missing, who can supply it, and which public uses are blocked until then? Create an evidence request instead of filling gaps with plausible stories. File: `customer-evidence/proof.md`.
- **evidence.libraries** (Core). Which VOC/customer-value themes, demo-call-flow, industry/news, competitor and coverage research libraries exist, and who owns their accepted outputs? Route to rich research and proof stores, not only a product-facts checker. File: `customer-evidence/research-libraries.md`.
- **evidence.earned-media** (Optional). Where is the record of backlinks, PR mentions, citations and peer shares, including date, exact URL, relevance, owner and any outreach limits? Observed links and mentions are evidence; a prospect list is not an earned placement. File: `customer-evidence/earned-media.md`.

## Channel scope and platform strategy (all engagements)
- **channels.active** (Core). Which channels are actually in scope? Use channel IDs from the list; an empty list means no channel production. Enable relevant branches rather than assuming every client needs every platform. File: `channels/strategy.md`.
- **channels.roles** (Core). For each selected channel, what audience, job, format, CTA and conversion does it serve? Give each platform a deliberate role. File: `channels/strategy.md`.
- **channels.cadence** (Core). What cadence and production capacity are realistic, and who creates each asset? Avoid promising volume without people or inputs. File: `channels/strategy.md`.
- **channels.inventory** (Core). Where are existing blog, newsletter, YouTube, LinkedIn and other selected-channel assets, inventories, editorial calendars and performance exports? Start from available material before making more. File: `channels/strategy.md`.
- **channels.repurpose** (Optional). Which source assets may be repurposed, who owns the original, and which derivatives are required or optional? Preserve source identity and avoid duplicating ownership. File: `channels/strategy.md`.
- **channels.reviewers** (Core). Who reviews each selected channel for audience fit, factual accuracy and final acceptance, and what happens when a reviewer is missing? A new audience must reach the actual worker and reviewer; never silently fall back to an old persona. File: `channels/strategy.md`.

## Measurement, sales feedback and reporting (all engagements)
- **performance.kpis** (Core). Which business outcomes, leading indicators and quality measures will be reported? Separate traffic, engagement, qualified demand, sales and retention. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.sources** (Core). Where are website analytics (for example GA4), search-console data (for example Google Search Console), CRM, sales-stage history and channel exports? Who owns definitions, access and refresh? Register actual sources rather than plausible connectors. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.attribution** (Optional). How are conversions defined, deduplicated and attributed, and what cannot be proven? Do not claim content caused revenue from correlation alone. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.windows** (Core). What reporting windows, baselines, lag and comparison rules apply? Avoid misleading period comparisons. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.feedback** (Optional). How will sales and customer success report lead quality, objections, wins and losses? Close the loop from content to commercial learning. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.reporting** (Core). Who receives which report, how often, and what decision should follow? Make reporting useful rather than another dashboard. File: `performance/measurement.md`.
- **performance.recurring-reports** (Core). Which recurring status reports and private meeting briefs are required, for whom, with what privacy, source, owner and review gate? A sales/conversion dashboard does not replace a written client report or meeting brief. File: `performance/reporting.md`.
- **performance.dictionary** (Core). Define the metrics we will compare across channels, including units, denominators, attribution, timezone, windows, lag and source-specific limits. Views, opens, clicks, qualified leads and sales need explicit definitions before cross-channel study. File: `performance/metrics-dictionary.md`.
- **performance.studies** (Optional). What bounded cross-channel question should we study, for which dates and cohort, and which owner outputs can support it? Create a small cited evidence pack rather than searching every client file or copying a data warehouse. File: `performance/cross-channel-studies.md`.
- **performance.keywords** (Optional). Where is the keyword bank, with query intent, audience, geography, volume/rank source, observed date and page/topic mapping? Keep measured query data separate from keyword ideas. File: `performance/search-and-ai.md`.
- **performance.ai-answers** (Optional). Where is the ChatGPT/AI-answer prompt and answer bank, with exact prompt, model/product, run date, locale, answer, cited URLs and repeat-check rule? One model answer is an observation under stated conditions, not universal visibility. File: `performance/search-and-ai.md`.

## Access, privacy, sources and authority (all engagements)
- **access.tools** (Core). Which tools and accounts exist, what read/write scopes are granted, and where are manual alternatives? Installed tools and subscriptions do not prove access. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **access.secrets** (Core). How will secure invitations and credentials be handled outside this workspace? Never ask for passwords, API keys, cookies or tokens in chat or Git. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **privacy.rules** (Core). Which data is confidential, restricted, consent-bound or subject to retention and deletion rules? Keep client and customer material inside its authorized private boundary. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **authority.actions** (Core). What may the assistant do locally, what needs review, and who alone can publish, send, spend or change production? Record desired delegation without granting it through the template. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **sources.locations** (Core). List the source files, documents, datasets and relevant public pages already available. Read supplied context first and ask only for missing information. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **sources.owners** (Core). Who maintains each source, how fresh must it be, and what is the evidence of its last successful refresh? A scheduled job or file timestamp is not a successful refresh. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **sources.conflicts** (Optional). What is the source priority when files disagree, and which unresolved questions need an owner decision? Keep conflicts visible instead of choosing the convenient copy. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **authority.regulated** (Optional). Which claims, regulated topics, endorsements, licenses, accessibility or legal reviews require a specialist? Route actual requirements to an appropriate reviewer; do not invent jurisdictional advice. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **authority.pilot** (Core). What one small, reversible task can prove the workspace and handoffs work before live integration? Separate setup checks from production reliability. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
- **authority.handoff** (Core). Who maintains this workspace, reviews changes and receives the handover? Give maintenance and offboarding an owner. File: `monitoring/authority.md`.
